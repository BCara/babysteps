Delete your account and data

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Delete in the app

  1. Open BabySteps and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and data.
  4. Enter your current password and confirm deletion.

If you are the sole caregiver, the app deletes your user profile, baby profile, feed/sleep/diaper events and Firebase Authentication account. This action cannot be undone.

Request deletion without the app

Email clb.bertram@googlemail.com from the address used for your BabySteps account. Use the subject “BabySteps account deletion”. We may ask you to verify control of the account before deleting data.

We aim to complete a verified request within 30 days and will confirm when it is complete.

What may remain temporarily

Security and operational logs, and copies in routine backups or recovery systems, may remain for a limited retention period before being overwritten or deleted. We may retain limited information where required by law or necessary to prevent fraud and protect the service.

If more than one caregiver can access a baby profile, contact support before deleting the account. The private test currently supports deletion for a sole-caregiver account and does not yet provide a caregiver-transfer workflow.
