Delete your account and data

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Delete in the app

  1. Open PitterPatter and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and data.
  4. Confirm your identity with your password or Google sign-in.

The app deletes your user profile and Firebase Authentication account. If another caregiver can access a baby profile, the app removes only your access and leaves the profile and care history available to them. If you are the last caregiver, that baby profile and its care records are deleted. This action cannot be undone.

Deleting a PitterPatter account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel Premium in Google Play before deleting the account if you do not want it to renew. PitterPatter removes its entitlement and purchase-token binding when the account is deleted, while Google Play may retain transaction records under its own requirements.

Request deletion without the app

Email codebertcreations@gmail.com from the address used for your PitterPatter account. Use the subject “PitterPatter account deletion”. We may ask you to verify control of the account before deleting data.

We aim to complete a verified request within 30 days and will confirm when it is complete.

What may remain temporarily

Security and operational logs, and copies in routine backups or recovery systems, may remain for a limited retention period before being overwritten or deleted. We may retain limited information where required by law or necessary to prevent fraud and protect the service.

You do not need to contact support before deleting a shared-caregiver account. PitterPatter automatically preserves shared baby profiles and care history for their remaining caregivers.
