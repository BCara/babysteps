Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 28 July 2026

This policy explains how the BabySteps Android app (“BabySteps”, “we”, “our”) handles information. BabySteps is intended for adult caregivers to record routine baby-care activity.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name and Firebase account identifier.
  • Baby profile information: the name or nickname and date of birth entered by a caregiver.
  • Care activity: feed, sleep and diaper events, timestamps, durations, optional quantities and optional notes.
  • Technical information: Firebase may process routine security, authentication, diagnostic and service-operation information such as IP address, device or app identifiers, and request logs.

How we use information

We use the information to authenticate users, store and synchronise care records, show timelines and summaries, support account and data deletion, prevent abuse, maintain security, diagnose faults and provide user support. We do not use care records for advertising, profiling or medical decision-making.

Service providers and sharing

BabySteps uses Google Firebase services for authentication, database storage and hosting. Google processes data on our behalf to provide these services. We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Storage and security

Data is transmitted using encrypted connections and stored in Firebase. Access rules restrict app data to authenticated, authorised caregivers. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so caregivers should avoid recording information that is not needed for routine care tracking.

Retention and deletion

Account and care information is retained while the account is active. A sole caregiver can delete the account and associated BabySteps data in the app from Settings. Deletion removes the user profile, baby profile, care events and authentication account. Operational logs or backups may persist for a limited period under Firebase retention and recovery processes before being overwritten or deleted. Legal or security requirements may require limited information to be retained for longer.

Deletion can also be requested using the instructions on the account deletion page.

Children’s information

BabySteps is directed to adult caregivers, not children. A caregiver may enter limited information about a child. The caregiver is responsible for having authority to provide and manage that information. The app should not be used by a child to create an account.

Your choices and rights

You may review and edit baby information and care entries in the app. You may delete entries or request deletion of the account and associated data. Depending on where you live, privacy law may provide additional access, correction, deletion, restriction or complaint rights. Contact us to exercise those rights.

International processing

Firebase’s service infrastructure and support operations may process information outside your state or country. We use Firebase’s Australia region for the primary Firestore database, but authentication, logs, backups or support systems may operate in other locations.

Medical disclaimer

BabySteps is a convenience log and is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, monitoring or emergency services. Do not rely on the app where a missed, delayed or incorrect record could cause harm.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made. Where appropriate, users will be notified in the app or through the test programme.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email clb.bertram@googlemail.com. Please use the subject “BabySteps privacy”.
