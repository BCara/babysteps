# Google Play Store Listing

## Main listing

**App name (30-character maximum)**

PitterPatter: Baby Care Log

**Short description (80-character maximum)**

Track feeds, sleep, nappies and the care records your family chooses in one simple timeline.

**Full description**

PitterPatter helps busy caregivers keep a simple record of everyday baby care.

Quickly log:

• Bottle and breast feeds  
• Wet, dirty or combined nappies  
• Pumping, solids, potty, tummy time and baths<br>
• Optional growth, milestone, temperature and medicine records<br>
• A customisable Home screen with the actions your family uses<br>
• Sleep start, finish and duration  
• Optional notes for your own reference

See recent activity in a clear timeline, check today’s totals and review a simple seven-day summary. A running sleep timer is stored securely so it remains available when you reopen the app.

Built for quick everyday use:

• Straightforward setup  
• Fast feed and nappy logging  
• Easy correction and removal of mistakes  
• Multiple baby profiles with active selection  
• Secure email/password or Google account
• Account and data deletion from Settings

PitterPatter can be used free with restrained banner advertising. An optional
auto-renewing PitterPatter Premium subscription removes all ads. Current prices
and billing periods are shown in the app by Google Play before purchase.
Premium can be managed or cancelled through Google Play.

### AI-enabled closed-test copy — release gated

Do not place the following copy in the public listing until consent, privacy,
Play Integrity-backed App Check, Data Safety answers and the exact tested AAB
all match:

> With your separate consent, the optional Assistant can summarise Today or the
> last seven days and answer a single question using only the selected baby's
> logged PitterPatter records. It shows the records supporting its response, is
> read-only and does not provide medical or general parenting advice. Free
> accounts include five lifetime generations; Premium and Founding Premium
> include 100 per calendar month.

Private test release

This is an early test version. The Free plan includes banner ads and no more than one full-screen ad per day after completing a log; Premium removes all ads. Features may change as we learn from real caregiver use. Please report problems through the support link in the app.

Important: PitterPatter is a convenience log, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, monitoring or emergency services. Do not rely on the app where a missed, delayed or incorrect record could cause harm. Contact an appropriate healthcare professional if you have concerns about a child’s health.
