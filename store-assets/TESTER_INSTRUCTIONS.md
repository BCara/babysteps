# BabySteps Closed-Test Instructions

Thank you for testing BabySteps. This is an early Android release for adult caregivers.

## Install

1. Open the Google Play opt-in link while signed into the invited Google account.
2. Choose **Become a tester**.
3. Install BabySteps from the Play Store link shown.
4. Keep yourself opted in for the full test period.
5. Enable normal Play Store updates.

## First session

1. Create an account.
2. Add a baby using a nickname or fictional test name if you prefer.
3. Log one feed, one nappy and one sleep.
4. Close and reopen the app.
5. Confirm the entries remain.

## What to use

Use the app naturally for feeds, sleep and nappies. Please try Timeline, Stats, editing and deletion at least once.

BabySteps is not a medical device and must not be relied on for medical decisions, monitoring or emergencies.

## Report a problem

Email `clb.bertram@googlemail.com` with:

- Phone model.
- Android version.
- BabySteps version shown by Google Play.
- What you did.
- What you expected.
- What happened.
- Whether it happened again.
- A screenshot if useful.

Avoid including a child’s real name, date of birth or sensitive note in a support email unless essential.

## Important

- Do not share your password.
- Use a unique test password.
- Keep at least one separate record if accurate care history is important to you.
- Delete disposable test records when finished.
- Account and data deletion is available in Settings and at <https://babysteps-cbert.web.app/delete-account.html>.
